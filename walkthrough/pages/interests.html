A simple creative process

  1. Prepare
    • Pick a place or a subject.
    • Think about the story.
  2. Photograph
    • Watch the direction of the light.
    • Simplify the background.
    • Wait for a good moment.
  3. Review
    • Choose the strongest frames.
    • Edit with restraint.
