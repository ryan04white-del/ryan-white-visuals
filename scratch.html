A camera can record almost anything. The real choice is deciding what deserves attention and what should be left outside the frame.

My process

Observation before equipment.

I’m drawn to photographs that preserve the atmosphere of a place rather than simply proving that I was there. Light, contrast, movement, and timing do more for a frame than carrying the biggest camera.

My Sony RX100 VII keeps the setup nimble. For video, DJI audio gear helps voices and ambient sound feel as intentional as the image.

  1. Prepare the story
    • Choose the feeling
    • Study the location
    • Pack only what helps
  2. Shape the frame
    • Find directional light
    • Simplify the background
    • Wait for the human moment
  3. Finish with restraint
    • Protect natural texture
    • Use contrast to direct attention
    • Cut anything that weakens the story
