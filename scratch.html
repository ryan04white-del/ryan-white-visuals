My process

From observation to final frame

I’m drawn to photographs that preserve the atmosphere of a place rather than simply documenting what was there. The camera matters, but patience and a clear point of view matter more.

My Sony RX100 VII is compact enough to keep nearby, and DJI audio gear helps video projects preserve voices and ambient sound without distracting from the moment.

  1. Prepare the story
    • Choose the feeling
    • Study the location
    • Pack only what helps
  2. Shape the frame
    • Find directional light
    • Simplify the background
    • Wait for the human moment
  3. Finish with restraint
    • Protect natural skin tones
    • Use color to reinforce mood
    • Cut anything that weakens the story
