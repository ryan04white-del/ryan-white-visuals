Working philosophy

The camera is only the beginning.

The strongest image is rarely the most complicated one. I try to notice first, simplify second, and only then press the shutter.

01

Notice

Watch the direction of light, the balance of the frame, and the small gesture that reveals character.

02

Simplify

Remove visual noise so the viewer knows where to look and what the moment is meant to feel like.

03

Preserve

Edit with restraint, protect the atmosphere, and keep the final image honest to the experience.
